# Katkee Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 November 2026

This policy explains what Katkee collects, how we use it, who can see it, how long we keep it
and the choices you have. It covers the Katkee apps for iPhone and Android, the website at
katkee.com and the service behind them.

## 1. Who we are

Katkee is operated by Rahul Katakdhond, a sole proprietorship registered in India ("Katkee",
"we", "us"). We decide how and why your personal data is used for the Katkee apps, the website
at katkee.com and the service they use, which makes us the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and the controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws elsewhere. Katkee is available worldwide; section 8 explains the
rights you have wherever you live.

Katkee is only for people 18 and older (section 9).

Questions about this policy or your data go to privacy@katkee.com. Section 11 has our other
contacts.

## 2. What we collect

### Account information
Username, email address, a securely hashed password (we never store your
actual password; see Section 6), display name, **date of birth**, and an
optional bio, interests (up to five), profile photo and cover photo.

If you sign in with your phone number, Twilio receives it to send you a
code. We keep only a keyed fingerprint of the number, not the number
itself, so we can recognise it when you sign in again. If you sign in with
Google, we keep your Google account ID and email address.

### Date of birth
We ask for your date of birth when you create an account, only to confirm
you're 18 or older (section 9). It's never shown on your profile or to
anyone else, never used for ads, and can't be changed in the app. If you
entered it wrongly, write to privacy@katkee.com to correct it.

### Content you create
Photos and videos you publish as Stories (with any sound they contain),
captions, stickers and drawings, Highlights you keep, comments, likes,
shares, and the direct messages you send (including photos and videos, and
Stories you share into a conversation). A photo or video you send in a message
can be opened only by you and the person you sent it to.

### How Katkee understands your Stories
So that you don't have to tag your Stories, Katkee works out what each one is
about. When you post a Story, our servers send its picture (for a video, its
cover frame), its caption, the words written on it and the name of its song
to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service. Claude gives back:

- its **topic**, from the same list of topics as Your interests (such as Food
  or Cricket), which we store with the Story and use to show it to people who
  enjoy that topic;
- a **description of the picture** for people who use a screen reader, which
  we keep with the Story unless you wrote your own;
- whether it may show content that breaks our rules, which we keep with the
  Story so our team can review it.

Anthropic processes your Story only to give us this answer, under terms that
don't allow it to train its AI models on it. Nothing else about you is sent:
not your name, username, account or who you are. If an older version of the
app let you choose a topic yourself, your choice is kept.

If you add a song to a Story, we record which song from Katkee's music
library and which part of it plays. The songs are openly licensed and credited
on the Story; choosing one tells us nothing else about you.

### Story stickers and Collab Stories
When you answer an interactive sticker on someone's Story, we keep what you
did: your vote on a poll or your answer to a quiz (one each, and it can't be
changed), the text you send to a question sticker ("Ask me anything"), and
whether you asked to be reminded when a countdown ends (we then send you one
notification at that time). If you post from someone's "Add Yours", we record
that your Story is part of that prompt's chain. If you invite someone to a
Collab Story, or are invited, we keep the invitation and whether it was
accepted. When you open a link sticker the app tells us a link was opened,
never which one; link stickers are checked against our link-safety rules and,
when it's configured, Google Safe Browsing.

When you change your profile photo, the app cuts out the square you choose
on your phone and uploads only that square. Your cover photo works the same
way: only the wide banner you frame is uploaded.

### Calls
Voice and video calls go straight between the two phones, encrypted between
them (WebRTC). When the phones can't reach each other directly, the call is
relayed by our service provider's relay servers (see section 5), still
encrypted: they can't hear or see it. We never record calls.

To set a call up, the two phones exchange connection details (their network
addresses and the formats they support) through our servers; we delete them
when the call ends. We keep the call log your chat shows ("Missed voice
call", "Video call · 4:12"): who called whom, when, whether it was a voice or
video call, how it ended and how long it lasted.

### Your connections
Who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, and who you've blocked or
muted.

### Your contacts (Contacts on Katkee, optional)
Only if you choose **Find friends from your contacts** and allow it on your
phone. Before anything is sent, the app turns each email address and phone
number in your contacts into a scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash) on your
phone. Only those codes are sent; names, photos, notes and the rest of your
contacts never leave your phone. We compare the codes with the verified
email addresses and phone numbers of accounts that allow being found this
way, show you the accounts that match, and then **forget the codes: we
never store your contacts or the codes made from them**, and never use
them for anything else (not for ads, suggestions or to contact anyone).

The codes are not encryption: someone who had one could work out the
phone number behind it by trying every number. That is why we don't keep
them. You can run the check again or never; turning off contacts for
Katkee in your phone's settings stops it.

Others can find you the same way, from your verified email or phone
number, unless you turn off **Let people find me from their contacts**
(Settings, then Privacy). They see your account, never your email or
number.

### Invites
Your personal invite link (katkee.com/i/...) shows your display name to
whoever opens it. If someone creates an account after opening your link, we
record that they joined from your invite, tell you, and suggest you to each
other. We don't track installs through the app stores and don't use any
attribution service.

### Your approximate area (Nearby Stories, optional)
Only if you turn on **Nearby Stories**. The app reads an approximate
location once in a while, turns it into a city-sized area (about
39 x 20 km) on your phone, and sends only that area, never your
coordinates. Your phone's **time zone** is also sent, so new Stories can
come from your region even if Nearby Stories is off.

We use the area and time zone only to show you Stories from people near
you and to show your public Stories to people near you. They are never
shown to anyone, never used for ads, and deleted:
- after 30 days without a refresh;
- at once when you turn Nearby Stories off (the area) or delete your
  account (both).

If you add a location sticker to a Story, the approximate place you choose
is shown on that Story.

### Activity used to rank Home and Story reach
We record views, how much of a Story was watched, likes, comments, shares,
profile visits, follows, quick skips, "Not interested" and "Show more like
this". We use them, and the interests you chose on your profile, for three
things:
- to order your Home;
- to decide how far a public Story reaches beyond its owner's followers;
- to give creators aggregate Insights.

For professional accounts' "When your followers are active" chart, we note
which half hours of the day you had Katkee open (not what you did), and keep
it for 8 days. A creator sees only each hour's share of their busiest hour,
never who was active, and only once at least 10 followers were.

### Interests we learn from your activity
To order your Home, Katkee also works out which topics you seem to like,
from the same list of topics you can choose on your profile. When you watch
a Story for a while or to the end, watch it again, like it, comment, share
it, visit its owner's profile or tap "Show more like this", the Story's topic (or, if it has none, the
interests its owner chose) counts a little more for you; a quick skip or
"Not interested" counts against it. This is a score per topic, nothing
more. It fades over time (it halves every 14 days), and how much one topic
can change in a day is limited. The interests you chose count too, but we
don't change them. We use what we learn only to order your Home: never to
infer anything about you beyond what topics of Stories you enjoy, and never
for Sponsored Stories, which use only the interests you chose. We never
learn Devotional from your activity, because it could reveal your religion;
it counts only if you choose it yourself.

You can see the topics Katkee thinks you like, and remove any of them, in
Settings, then Your interests. Removing a topic resets it and keeps it out
for 30 days. Nobody else can see your learned interests, not even the
people whose Stories you watch.

A Story's view count is public. Only the Story's owner can see who viewed it.

### Sponsored Stories
Katkee shows Sponsored Stories, labelled "Sponsored", from advertisers. Which
ones you see depends on your interests and your phone's platform (iPhone or
Android). Advertisers receive only totals, such as how many times an ad was
shown or tapped, never who you are. Your messages, area, time zone and date
of birth are never used for ads. We don't use advertising identifiers or track you
across other companies' apps and websites.

### App usage and crashes
Katkee's own analytics record when the app is opened, which platform and
app version you use, and crash counts. We use them to measure how many
people use Katkee each day and month, and how stable it is.

When the app crashes, it sends Katkee a crash report: the error, the
technical trace of where in the app's code it happened, the screen you
were on, your phone's model and system version, and the app version,
linked to your account. A crash report never contains your messages,
captions, photos or videos. We use crash reports only to find and fix
bugs. Crashes of the app's native code are also collected by Google Play
and Apple, under their own privacy policies, and shown to us without
your identity.

We don't use third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

### Feedback you send
Settings > Send feedback sends us what you write, and a screenshot only if
you choose to attach one, with your username, app version, phone model
and system version. Only Katkee staff who handle support can see it. We
use it to answer you and to improve Katkee.

### Verification requests (optional)
If you ask for a verified badge (Settings > Request verification), we
collect the category you choose, your full name as it appears on your ID,
the links you add, and a **photo of a government-issued ID**. We use them
only to check that the account really is you, or the brand or organisation
it represents, and to decide on the badge. Only Katkee staff who review
verification requests can see the ID photo; it is never shown to anyone
else, including other users, and every time staff open it, it is recorded
in our audit log. The ID photo is deleted 30 days after we approve or deny
the request, or after you withdraw it, and at once if you delete your
account. Whether you're verified, and as what (for example "Creator"), is
shown on your profile.

### Reports
If you report a Story, comment, message or account, we keep a record of the
report, what it was about, and how a moderator resolved it. A reported
message is kept as evidence for the moderator.

### Technical information
- Your IP address: to limit abuse and secure accounts. It also appears in
  server logs, which Indian law requires us to keep for 180 days (Katkee is
  run from India; see section 5).
- Device and app details for each signed-in session, so you can sign out a
  device.
- The push token Apple or Google gives your phone, so we can send
  notifications.
- When you visit katkee.com, our servers log your IP address and the page
  you asked for, as any website does. The website sets no cookies and has
  no analytics or advertising trackers. When someone opens a web profile or
  a Story on katkee.com, we count only how many such pages were opened each
  day, not who opened them or whose profile it was.

### What we do **not** collect or do
- We don't access your contacts unless you choose Contacts on Katkee, and
  then we don't keep them (see above). We never access your precise
  location.
- We remove location details (EXIF/GPS and similar metadata) from the copies
  of your photos and videos that we show, and delete uploaded originals 30
  days after processing.
- We don't sell your data, use your content to train third-party AI models,
  or share it with data brokers.

## 3. How we use it

- **To operate Katkee**: your account, Stories and Highlights,
  notifications, direct messages, search and suggestions.
- **To rank and reach**: Home and how far public Stories reach, as
  described above.
- **To keep Katkee safe**: rate limits, link checks, and acting on reports.
  A moderator can remove content or restrict, suspend or disable an
  account, and a suspended account is signed out everywhere.
- **For Sponsored Stories and analytics**, as described above.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Stories**: a public account's Stories can be seen by anyone, and reach
  people beyond its followers as described above. A private account's
  Stories are seen only by followers it approved. Followers-only Stories
  are seen only by followers.
- **Direct messages**: only the people in the conversation see them. Staff
  can't browse messages. A moderator sees a message only when someone
  reports it.
- **Calls**: only the two people on the call hear and see it. The call's
  line in your chat is visible to the two of you, like a message.
- **Profile photo and cover photo**: anyone signed in who can see your
  profile, including when your account is private. People you've blocked,
  or who've blocked you, never see them.
- **Your profile on the web**: if your account is public, anyone can see
  your profile at katkee.com/u/your-username without the app or signing in:
  your name, username, photo, cover photo, verified badge, professional
  category (if you show it), bio, interests, follower and following counts,
  the Highlights and Stories you shared with everyone, and their captions,
  text, stickers and music credit. Search engines may list your profile.
  Followers-only and Close Friends Stories, likes, comments, viewers and
  view counts never appear there, and watching a Story on the web doesn't
  count as a view. Turn off **Show my profile on the web** (Settings, then
  Privacy) to keep only a plain "Open in Katkee" page at that address; a
  private, suspended or deleted account always has only that page. Changes
  take effect within a few minutes, though search engines may take longer
  to update what they show.
- **Blocking**: hides your content from someone and theirs from you, both
  ways, at once.
- **Sticker answers**: who voted on a poll or quiz and what they chose, and
  answers to a question sticker, are seen only by the Story's owner. Other
  viewers see a poll's percentages after they vote, and a quiz's answer after
  they answer. If the owner shares an answer to a new Story, it shows the
  question and the answer, not who sent it.
- **Collab Stories**: once accepted, a Collab Story shows both names and
  reaches both people's followers, within the owner's own privacy settings (a
  private account's Collab Story is still seen only by its followers).
  Neither person's blocks are bypassed, and either can end the collab.
- **Add Yours**: the Stories in a chain are shown only to people who could
  already see each of them.
- **Your area and time zone**: never shown to anyone.
- **Interests we learn**: only you see them (Settings, then Your interests).
- **Verification ID photo**: only staff who review verification requests,
  each view recorded in the audit log.

## 5. Service providers

- **Amazon Web Services**: our servers, database, file storage and email.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** and **Apple Push Notification
  service**: deliver notifications.
- **Twilio**: sends phone sign-in codes, if you use phone sign-in, and
  relays calls that can't connect directly. To relay a call it sees the
  network addresses of the two phones, never what's said or shown.
- **Google**: Google sign-in, if you use it, and Safe Browsing checks of
  links posted in Katkee.
- **Anthropic**: its AI service, Claude, works out each Story's topic and
  writes a description of its picture (see "How Katkee understands your
  Stories" above).

They process data only to provide these services to us. Our servers,
database and files are in India (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai); Twilio,
Google, Apple and Anthropic may process the data they handle for us outside
India.

**Wherever you use Katkee from, your data is stored and processed in India.**
If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you give it
to us directly when you use Katkee, and India is not a country the European
Commission or the UK has found to give adequate protection. We protect it as
this policy describes wherever it is, and where a service provider moves it
on, we rely on that provider's Standard Contractual Clauses (or the UK
Addendum) or other safeguards the law recognises. Ask privacy@katkee.com for
a copy of them.

## 6. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; we can't recover your actual password.
Sessions use short-lived signed access tokens and an individually revocable
refresh token per device. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and our
database and file storage are encrypted at rest. Staff access to the admin
console needs two-factor authentication, and every admin action is
recorded in an audit log that can't be edited.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Stories**: disappear from view after 24 hours. Highlights you keep
  stay until you remove them.
- **Deleted Stories**: their media is purged after 30 days, unless an open
  report still needs it.
- **Deleted accounts**:
  - unavailable at once;
  - personal data purged and the account scrubbed after 30 days;
  - content removed by moderation kept up to 180 days as evidence;
  - the details you registered with (username and any earlier usernames,
    email address, display name, date of birth and sign-in identifiers)
    kept separately for 180 days after deletion, because India's IT Rules
    2021 (rule 3(1)(h)) require it, then deleted. We use them only to
    answer lawful requests.
- **Sticker answers, reminders and collab invitations**: kept with the Story
  they belong to, deleted with it, and deleted when the account that sent
  them is deleted.
- **Uploads never used**: deleted after 48 hours.
- **Photo originals**: deleted 30 days after processing.
- **A video of your Story prepared for sharing to another app**: deleted
  24 hours after you asked for it.
- **Your area**: see above.
- **Interests we learn**: each topic fades over time and is deleted 180 days
  after it last changed; all of them are deleted at once when you delete
  your account.
- **Raw analytics events**: deleted after 90 days; only daily totals that
  identify nobody remain.
- **Crash reports**: deleted after 90 days.
- **Calls**: the connection details are deleted when the call ends. The
  call's line in your chat stays like a message; our record of the call
  (who, when, how it ended, how long) is deleted 90 days after it ended.
- **Verification ID photo**: deleted 30 days after the request is approved,
  denied or withdrawn, and at once if you delete your account. The request
  itself (category, name and links) is kept while your account exists, so
  we can review your badge.
- **Feedback**: kept until we've dealt with it, then deleted after 180
  days. Deleting your account deletes your feedback and crash reports.
- **Security records**: up to 400 days.
- **Server logs**: 180 days, kept in India, as the CERT-In directions of
  2022 require.
- **Backups**: the database can be restored to any moment in the last
  14 days. Daily backups of the database and stored files are kept for
  35 days and monthly backups for a year; anything you deleted is gone
  from backups when they expire.
- **Earlier versions of stored files**: 30 days after a file is deleted.

## 8. Your choices and rights

In the app you can:
- edit your profile;
- make your account private;
- turn Nearby Stories on or off (Settings, then Privacy);
- choose whether people can find you from their contacts (Settings, then
  Privacy);
- choose whether your public profile shows on the web (Settings, then
  Privacy);
- see and remove the interests Katkee has learned (Settings, then Your
  interests);
- turn notification types on or off;
- block, mute, report, or delete your own Stories and comments;
- **delete your account** (Settings, then Delete account).

If you can't use the app, you can ask us to delete your account at
[katkee.com/delete-account](https://katkee.com/delete-account). Write to
privacy@katkee.com for a copy of your data or anything else.

In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 gives you the right
to:
- get a summary of your personal data and how we use it;
- have it corrected, completed, updated or erased;
- have your grievances about it answered;
- nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't use
  them yourself.

Write to privacy@katkee.com to use any of them. If you're not satisfied
with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
If you'd like this policy in Hindi or another language listed in the
Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India, ask us at the same address.

### Wherever you live
You can ask us, at privacy@katkee.com, for a copy of your data, to correct
it, to delete it, or to stop or limit something we do with it. We answer
within a month (within 45 days for California), we don't charge for it, and
using a right never changes how Katkee treats you. We may ask you to confirm
the request from your account's email address so no one else can make it.

### European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)
You have the right to access, correct, delete and move (port) your data, to
object to our use of it, to restrict it, and to withdraw any consent at any
time. The legal bases we rely on:
- **Contract**: running your account, Stories, Highlights, messages and
  notifications, which you asked for by signing up (Article 6(1)(b)).
- **Legitimate interests**: ordering Home (including the interests we learn)
  and how far public Stories reach, choosing Sponsored Stories from the interests you chose, keeping
  Katkee safe (rate limits, link checks, moderation), security records,
  and our own analytics, which identify nobody once counted
  (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object to any of these; for Sponsored Stories,
  removing your interests in Edit Profile stops them being used.
- **Consent**: Nearby Stories (your area), a location sticker, and Contacts
  on Katkee (checked once each time you choose it, and not kept); you can
  turn them off at any time.
- **Legal obligation**: keeping server logs and the details of deleted
  accounts for as long as the law requires (section 7).

You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work
(in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

### United States (California and other states)
In the last 12 months we collected the categories in section 2 (identifiers,
account details, your content and activity on Katkee, approximate area if you
turned it on, and device information), from you and your use of Katkee, for
the purposes in section 3. **We don't sell your personal information or share
it for cross-context behavioural advertising**, and we don't use sensitive
personal information to infer things about you. You have the right to know,
correct and delete your data, and to opt out of sale or sharing (which we
don't do). You can use an authorised agent.

### Other countries
Data protection laws elsewhere (for example Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA,
Singapore's PDPA, Australia's Privacy Act, Japan's APPI and South Korea's
PIPA) give similar rights: to know what we hold, to correct or delete it, and
to complain to your regulator. Write to privacy@katkee.com and we'll help.

## 9. Katkee is for adults (18 and older)

Katkee is only for people 18 and older. That's true in every country, even
where the law would allow younger people to sign up. Under India's Digital
Personal Data Protection Act 2023, anyone under 18 is a child, and we don't
knowingly collect personal data from children anywhere.

- When you create an account, we ask for your date of birth. If it shows
  you're under 18, we don't create the account and keep nothing you
  entered. If you started with Google or a phone number, that verified
  sign-in is deleted at once.
- If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.
- To tell us about an account that may belong to someone under 18, report
  it in the app (reason: May be under 18) or write to grievance@katkee.com.
