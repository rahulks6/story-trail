## 5. Content moderation and enforcement

Katkee has real, working tools for this, not a policy with nothing behind
it:

- **Reporting**: any Story, comment, or account can be reported by any
  user, for a real reason (spam, harassment, nudity, violence, hate
  speech, self-harm, or other).
- **Review**: reports go to a real moderator queue; a moderator can
  remove the reported content or suspend the account.
- **Suspension**: a suspended account is immediately signed out
  everywhere (every active session is revoked) and can't log back in
  until the suspension is lifted.
- **Blocking**: you can block anyone; it immediately hides your content
  from them and theirs from you, in both directions.

We can remove content or suspend/terminate accounts that violate these
Terms, at our discretion, with or without notice.

## 6. Direct messages

Messages are private between participants. We don't read them except as
necessary to operate the service (e.g. investigating a report filed
against a message) or as required by law.

## 7. Highlights and your Archive

Stories normally expire 24 hours after posting. Adding one to a
Highlight keeps it visible past that expiry, on your profile, until you
remove it or delete the Highlight. Deleting a Story removes it
everywhere, including from every Highlight it was ever part of — that
deletion is permanent.

## 8. Termination

You can delete your own account at any time, in-app, under Profile →
Delete account (password-confirmed, since it's permanent). We may
suspend or terminate your account for violating these Terms, operating
multiple accounts to evade a suspension, or activity that risks the
service or other users.
