# Katkee in Hindi and Marathi: glossary

The words the app uses for its own features, so every screen says the same thing. Like WhatsApp and
Instagram in Hindi and Marathi: short and friendly, everyday words, and the English word in Devanagari
wherever that is what people actually say ("स्टोरी", not "कहानी"). When a new string needs one of
these, use the word here; when it needs a new feature word, add it here first.

## Conventions

- **Katkee** stays in Latin letters in every language. So do usernames (@asha) and anything people wrote.
- **You**: Hindi uses आप (आपकी स्टोरी), Marathi uses तुम्ही (तुमची स्टोरी). Buttons are polite imperatives:
  Hindi "…करें", Marathi "…करा".
- **Full stop**: Hindi ends sentences with । (purna viram); Marathi with a full stop (.), as Marathi print does.
- **Digits** are always Latin (0-9), as in Indian apps; numbers are grouped the Indian way
  (1,00,000). Big counts on profiles use हज़ार / लाख / करोड़ (Marathi हजार / लाख / कोटी), e.g. 12.3 हज़ार.
- **Plurals**: Hindi and Marathi entries still have `one` and `other`; Hindi's `one` covers 0 and 1.
- **Nukta**: Hindi writes the nukta in loanwords (फ़ॉलो, फ़ोटो, ज़्यादा); Marathi does not (फॉलो, फोटो).
- Placeholders ({name}, {count}) and marked parts (`<terms>…</terms>`) are kept exactly; move them
  wherever the sentence needs them.
