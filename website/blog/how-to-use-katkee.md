# How to Use Katkee: A Beginner's Guide

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

New to Katkee? This guide takes you from download to your first Story in a few minutes, and shows the features most people miss in their first week.

## 1. Sign up

1. Install **Katkee** from Google Play.
2. Tap **Continue with Google** and choose your account. You can also sign up with your email.
3. Choose a **username**. Katkee suggests one from your name; you can change it.
4. Enter your **date of birth**. Katkee is for people 18 and older, and your birthday is never shown on your profile.

If a friend sent you an invite link, open it before you sign up: your friend is told you joined, and you're suggested to each other.

## 2. Set up your profile

Katkee walks you through a few quick steps:

- **Add a profile photo.** Friends recognise you faster in Stories, chats and search. Choose a photo or take a selfie.
- **Follow people.** Katkee suggests people to follow, including friends from your contacts who are already on Katkee (only if you allow it). Their Stories will show on your Home.

You can edit your name, bio, photo and cover photo anytime from your profile.

## 3. Watch Stories

Your **Home** shows Stories from the people you follow. Swipe up for the next one. Tap the heart to like, or reply to start a chat. When you've seen everything, Katkee shows popular Highlights and people you might want to follow.

## 4. Post your first Story

1. Tap the **Create** button.
2. Take a photo or video, or choose one from your gallery.
3. Add a **song** from the music library and pick the part you like.
4. Add text, emoji, GIFs or stickers. Long-press anything you added to change or remove it, or drag it to the bin.
5. Tap share and choose who sees it: **Everyone**, **Followers** or **Close Friends**.

Your Story stays for 24 hours. To see who viewed it, open your own Story and tap the views count at the bottom.

Out of ideas? Every evening, Katkee suggests a theme of the day on Home. Our [70 status ideas for every mood](/blog/status-ideas) help too.

## 5. Make a Close Friends list

Go to **Settings > Close Friends** and add the people you trust most. When you share to Close Friends, only they see it. Only you can see the list, and nobody is told when you add or remove them. More in [how to make your Close Friends list](/blog/katkee-close-friends).

## 6. Chat and call

Tap the **messages** icon to see your chats. You can:

- send messages, photos and voice notes
- react to messages with emoji
- make group chats
- start voice or video calls

Replies to your Stories appear here too.

## 7. Keep your favourites as Highlights

On your profile, tap **New** in the Highlights row to collect Stories into a Highlight, like "Goa trip" or "Diwali 2026". Friends can open, like and comment on your Highlights. See [Katkee Highlights](/blog/katkee-highlights).

## 8. Invite your friends

Katkee is better with your people on it. Go to **Settings > Invite friends** to share your personal invite link on WhatsApp or anywhere else. When a friend joins from your link, you're told, and you're suggested to each other.

## Settings worth knowing

- **Settings > Notifications:** choose which notifications you get.
- **Settings > Language:** change the app's language.
- **Settings > Appearance:** light or dark look.
- **Settings > Blocked accounts** and **Muted accounts:** manage who you've blocked or muted.
- **Settings > Account and sign-in:** add two-step login for extra security.
- **Settings > Download your data:** get a copy of everything you've shared.
