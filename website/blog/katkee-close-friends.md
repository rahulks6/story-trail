# How to Make a Close Friends List on Katkee

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

Some Stories are for everyone. Others are only for the people who'd laugh at your messy room, understand your inside jokes and reply with a voice note. That's what **Close Friends** on Katkee is for.

## What Close Friends does

Your Close Friends list is a group of people you choose. When you share a Story to **Close Friends**, only the people on that list can see it. Everyone else, including your other followers, doesn't see it at all.

- **Only you can see the list.** Nobody can see who else is on it.
- **Nobody is told** when you add or remove them.
- Your friends see a small **Close Friends** badge on Stories you share with them, so they know it's just for them.

## How to make your list

1. Open **Settings** from your profile.
2. Tap **Close Friends**.
3. Search for people, or pick from **Your followers**.
4. Tap **Add** next to each person.

You can come back anytime to add or remove people. The number at the top shows how many people are on your list.

## How to share a Story with Close Friends

1. Create your Story as usual: photo or video, music, text and stickers.
2. When you're ready to share, choose **Close Friends** instead of Everyone or Followers.
3. Share. Only the people on your list will see it.

You choose the audience for every Story separately, so you can share one Story with everyone and the next with only your Close Friends.

## Tips for a good Close Friends list

- **Keep it small.** The fewer people on it, the more freely you'll share. Many people keep it under 20.
- **Review it now and then.** Friendships change; update your list every few months.
- **Use it often.** Close Friends Stories don't need to be perfect. Post the small, honest moments.
- **Reply to theirs.** It works best when everyone in the group shares.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can people tell they're not on my list?** No. People who aren't on your list don't see your Close Friends Stories at all, and they're never told they were left out.

**Does Close Friends work with a public account?** Yes. Even if your account is public, Close Friends Stories go only to your list.

**Can I see who viewed my Close Friends Story?** Yes, just like any of your Stories: open it and tap the views count at the bottom.

**Can I add someone who doesn't follow me?** Yes, you can search for anyone on Katkee and add them.
