# Close Friends Story Ideas: 40 Things to Post for Your Inner Circle

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

Most of us post less than we'd like to, because posting for everyone feels like performing. Your college seniors, your relatives and that person you met once all see it. A close friends Story is different. It's for the 5, 10 or 20 people who actually know you. That changes what you can post, and how it feels.

Here are 40 ideas for Stories that only your inner circle sees.

## Honest, everyday you

1. Your real morning face, before coffee
2. The messy room you'd never show anyone else
3. What you're eating at 1 am
4. Your study or work setup, with the 14 open tabs
5. Your face after a long day: *"That's it, that's the update"*
6. The outfit you're not sure about: *"Yes or no?"*
7. A rant about something small, in 15 seconds
8. Your new haircut, before anyone else sees it
9. Your playlist right now
10. The book, show or reel you can't stop thinking about

## Inside jokes and memories

11. A throwback only your friends will understand
12. A meme that is exactly about one of them (tag them)
13. The place where something funny happened years ago
14. A voice note-style video telling a story from today
15. Your group's "official" photo pose, recreated
16. A blurry photo from last night's plan
17. Your friend asleep in the car (with permission later)
18. Screenshots of an old chat that still makes you laugh
19. "Guess where I am" with a close-up clue
20. A rating of your friend's cooking, out of 10

## Asking your people

Close friends are the best people to ask. They'll actually reply.

21. "Which one?" with two outfits, cakes or phone covers
22. "Need a song for this mood" with a sky photo
23. "Recommend me a movie for tonight"
24. "Who's free this weekend?"
25. "Rate my rangoli / drawing / cooking"
26. "Help me name my plant"
27. "Is it too late for chai?"
28. "What should I write in this birthday card?"
29. "Should I cut my hair short?"
30. "Plan for Sunday: go out or sleep?"

## Feelings you wouldn't post publicly

31. A bad day, said simply: *"Rough one. Will be fine."*
32. A small win nobody else would care about
33. Something you're nervous about tomorrow
34. A thank-you to one friend, without naming them
35. A song that says how you feel
36. Missing home, with a photo of the train ticket
37. A goal you're working on, privately
38. A memory of someone you've lost
39. Something you're proud of but shy to say out loud
40. A simple *"Love you guys"* with your group photo

## Tips for close friends Stories

- **Keep the list small.** If it has 150 people, it's not close friends anymore. Review it every few months.
- **Post more often, with less effort.** These Stories don't need to be perfect.
- **Reply to theirs.** Close friends Stories work when everyone joins in.
- **Add a song.** It sets the mood in one second. Here's [how to add a song to your Story or status](/blog/add-song-to-story).

## How to post to close friends

On Instagram, you can make a Close Friends list and share a Story to it with the green star. On WhatsApp, status privacy lets you choose "Only share with…" a list of contacts. We compare the options in [ways to share Stories only with close friends](/blog/share-stories-with-close-friends).

On **Katkee**, choosing who sees each Story is built in. Before you share, pick **Everyone**, **Followers** or **Close Friends**. Only you can see who's on your Close Friends list; your friends see a small Close Friends badge on Stories shared with them. Katkee also has chats, voice notes and video calls, so when a friend replies to your Story, the conversation is right there.
