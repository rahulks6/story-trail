# Who Can See Your Status? A Simple Privacy Guide

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026

You post a quick status from a friend's wedding, and suddenly your boss, an old classmate and your aunt have all seen it. It happens to everyone. The good news is that every major app lets you choose who sees your status and Stories. Here's how, in plain words.

## WhatsApp

**Who sees it by default:** everyone saved in your phone contacts who also has your number saved.

**How to change it:** open **Settings > Privacy > Status**. You'll see three options:

- **My contacts:** everyone in your contacts.
- **My contacts except…:** everyone except the people you pick. Good for hiding status from a few people.
- **Only share with…:** only the people you pick. Good for a small, trusted group.

**Good to know:**

- The change applies to status updates you post after you change it, not ones already posted.
- If you mute someone's status, they can still see yours. Muting only hides theirs from you.
- Your status disappears after 24 hours.

## Instagram

**Who sees it by default:** all your followers, or everyone if your account is public.

**Ways to limit it:**

- **Close Friends:** make a list and share a Story only to them.
- **Hide Story from:** in Story settings, pick people who won't see any of your Stories.
- **Private account:** only approved followers see your posts and Stories.

## Katkee

**Who sees it:** you choose for every Story, right before you share it.

- **Everyone:** anyone on Katkee can see it, and it can appear for people discovering new Stories.
- **Followers:** only people who follow you.
- **Close Friends:** only the people on your Close Friends list. Only you can see the whole list; your friends see a Close Friends badge on Stories shared with them.

**More controls:**

- **Private account:** people must ask to follow you, and you approve them.
- **Block** someone and they can't see your profile or Stories, or message you.
- **Mute** someone to stop seeing their Stories without unfollowing.
- See **who viewed** each of your Stories.
- Stories disappear after 24 hours unless you add them to a **Highlight** on your profile.

## Five habits that keep your status private

1. **Check the audience before you post.** It takes one second and saves the awkward moments.
2. **Keep a small close friends list.** Review it every few months; people drift.
3. **Don't post your location live.** Share where you were after you've left.
4. **Think about the background.** Addresses, number plates, documents and screens are easy to miss.
5. **Ask before posting other people.** Especially photos of friends' children, or anyone who might not want to be seen.
