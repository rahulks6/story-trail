# Founding Member Badge: Be One of Katkee's First 1,000

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

Every community has its first people, the ones who were there before everyone else. On Katkee, the first 1,000 people to join get a **Founding member** badge on their profile, with their number, for good.

## What is the Founding member badge?

It's a small badge on your Katkee profile that says **Founding member** with your number, like *Founding member #57*. Your number is your place in line: #1 was the first person to join, #1,000 will be the last.

- It's **free**. You don't buy it or apply for it.
- It's **permanent**. It stays on your profile as long as you have your account.
- It's **numbered**. Nobody else will ever have your number.

## How to get it

1. Download Katkee from Google Play.
2. Create your account.

That's it. If you're among the first 1,000 real accounts, the badge appears on your profile automatically, and Katkee shows you your number the first time you open the app.

## How many spots are left?

When you get your badge, Katkee shows how many of the 1,000 spots are still open. Once they're all taken, the badge closes for good and no new account can get one.

## Why we're doing this

Katkee is new, and the people who join now are shaping it. They post the first Stories, send us feedback, find the bugs and bring their friends. The badge is our way of saying thank you, and of remembering who was here first.

## Bring your friends before the spots run out

Want your friends to have one too? Share your invite link from **Settings > Invite friends**. If they join while spots are left, they'll get their own number.

While you're at it, there's a running [invite contest](/blog/katkee-invite-contest): bring the most friends by 21 December 2026 and win a ₹1,000 Amazon voucher.
