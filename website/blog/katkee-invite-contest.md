# Katkee Invite Contest: Win a ₹1,000 Amazon Voucher

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

Katkee is better when your friends are on it, so we're running a contest. **Whoever brings the most friends to Katkee with their invite link between 23 November and 21 December 2026 wins a ₹1,000 Amazon.in gift voucher.**

## How to take part

1. Open Katkee and tap the **🏆 trophy** on Home, or go to **Settings > Invite contest**.
2. Tap **Invite on WhatsApp**, or share your invite link anywhere else.
3. Your friend opens your link, installs Katkee and signs up.
4. Your friend counts for you once they've used Katkee on **3 different days**.

The contest screen shows each friend's progress (for example, *2 of 3 days*), how many friends count for you, your place, and the top 10.

## Why "3 different days"?

So that the contest rewards real friends, not empty accounts. A friend who signs up and uses Katkee for a few days counts. An account made just to raise someone's number doesn't.

## Key dates

- **23 November 2026:** the contest starts
- **21 December 2026:** last day for friends to join from your link
- **24 December 2026:** last day their days on Katkee count
- **By 26 December 2026:** the winner is announced in the app

All dates are India time.

## Who can take part

Anyone with a Katkee account who lives in India. Katkee is for people 18 and older. Members of the Katkee team can't take part.

## Tips to bring more friends

- **Start with your closest group.** A college or office group that joins together actually uses Katkee, so they count faster.
- **Share where your friends are:** WhatsApp groups, your Instagram bio or Story, your status.
- **Post your own Stories.** Friends join when they see something worth joining for.
- **Remind them to open it.** A friend who signs up but never comes back won't count. A quick "check my Story" helps.

## Fair play

Fake accounts, using other people's phones or emails, or paying people to sign up will get you disqualified. We check the winner's friends before sending the prize.
