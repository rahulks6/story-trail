# Apps Like Instagram Without Reels: 5 Private Apps for Close Friends

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

Instagram started as a place to see what your friends were up to. Today, open it and you're more likely to see Reels from strangers, creators and ads than a photo from your college friend. If you're looking for an app like Instagram but private, without Reels and without the pressure, here are five options and what each one is good at.

## What to look for in a private social app

Before you switch, decide what you actually want:

- **A small audience:** posts that go to people you choose, not to strangers.
- **No endless feed:** you see your friends, then you're done.
- **Easy posting:** something quick enough that you'll actually use it every day.
- **Your friends on it:** the best private app is the one your friends will join.

## 1. Katkee: Stories with music, for your own people

Katkee is a free Stories app built around small audiences, made in India.

- **Choose who sees every Story:** Everyone, Followers or Close Friends, decided each time you share.
- **No Reels feed:** Home shows Stories from the people you follow first; suggestions only appear once you've watched them all.
- **Music on Stories:** add a song from a library sorted by mood and pick the part that plays.
- **Chats, voice notes and video calls** with the same people.
- **Highlights** to keep your best Stories on your profile.
- In **English, Hindi, Marathi** and six more languages.

**Good for:** sharing your day with your gang without performing for everyone. **Keep in mind:** it's new, so invite your friends with your link from Settings > Invite friends. Free on Android; for people 18 and older. Learn more in [what Katkee is](/blog/what-is-katkee).

## 2. BeReal: one real photo a day

BeReal sends everyone a notification at a random time each day, and you post a photo from your front and back camera within a few minutes.

**Good for:** honest, unfiltered moments with friends. **Keep in mind:** it's one post a day at a time the app chooses, and there's no music or chat in the way you'd use WhatsApp.

## 3. Locket Widget: photos on your friends' home screen

Locket puts a widget on your home screen. When a friend sends a photo, it appears right there.

**Good for:** couples and a few best friends who want to feel close every day. **Keep in mind:** it's made for a small circle and quick photos, not Stories, profiles or longer conversations.

## 4. WhatsApp Status

Your status goes to your phone contacts, and you can choose **Only share with…** to limit it to a list. Here's [how to hide your WhatsApp status from someone](/blog/hide-whatsapp-status).

**Good for:** reaching family and friends who are already on WhatsApp. **Keep in mind:** it's tied to your contacts list, and the setting applies to every update until you change it.

## 5. Snapchat private Stories

Snapchat lets you make a private Story that only the friends you pick can see.

**Good for:** friend groups who already chat on Snapchat. **Keep in mind:** the app also has its own feed of public videos, and it only works if your friends are on Snapchat.

## Can you use Instagram without Reels?

You can't switch Reels off completely, but you can make Instagram calmer:

- **Following feed:** tap the Instagram logo at the top of Home and choose **Following** to see posts only from accounts you follow, newest first.
- **Close Friends:** share Stories only with your Close Friends list.
- **Private account:** only approved followers see your posts and Stories.
- **Mute and unfollow** accounts that make you feel worse, not better.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Stories with music, an audience for each post, and chat:** Katkee
- **One unplanned photo a day:** BeReal
- **Photos on your best friend's home screen:** Locket
- **Status for family who only use WhatsApp:** WhatsApp Status
- **A private Story for a Snapchat group:** Snapchat

Whatever you choose, the trick is the same: pick a small group, post small moments often, and reply to theirs. For ideas, see our [close friends Story ideas](/blog/close-friends-story-ideas).
