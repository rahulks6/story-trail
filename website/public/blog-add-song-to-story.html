How to Add a Song to Your Story or Status

23 November 2026

A photo of the evening sky is nice. The same photo with the right song is a mood. Adding music is the easiest way to make a Story or status feel like you. Here's how to do it on Instagram, WhatsApp and Katkee, plus tips on choosing the part of the song that fits.

On Instagram

  1. Open the camera from your feed or profile and take or pick a photo or video.
  2. Tap the sticker icon at the top and choose Music.
  3. Search for a song or pick one from the suggestions.
  4. Drag along the song to choose the part you want, and pick a style: lyrics, the album cover, or just the song name.
  5. Tap Done, then share to Your Story or Close Friends.

If you don't see the Music sticker, update the app. Some songs aren't available in every country, and business accounts may have a smaller library.

On WhatsApp

WhatsApp has been adding music to status updates. To try it:

  1. Go to the Updates tab and tap the camera or pencil to add a status.
  2. Pick a photo or video.
  3. Look for a music icon at the top of the editor. If you see it, tap it, search for a song and choose the part you want.
  4. Tap Send and choose who can see it.

If there's no music icon yet, update WhatsApp from the Play Store. Features often reach different phones at different times. Until then, a simple workaround is to record a short video with the song playing in the background.

On Katkee

Katkee is built around Stories with music, so the song is part of the editor from the start.

  1. Tap Create and take a photo or video, or pick one from your gallery.
  2. Tap the music button and browse songs by mood: Happy, Chill, Fun, Energetic, Romantic and more.
  3. Choose the part of the song you want; you'll hear it as you move it.
  4. Add text, emoji or stickers if you like.
  5. Choose who sees it: Everyone, Followers or Close Friends, then share.

Katkee's songs come from a library of more than 100 tracks licensed for sharing, and your friends see the song name on your Story.

Choosing the right part of a song

The song matters less than the 15 seconds you pick. A few tips:

  • Start where the feeling starts. Skip long intros. Jump to the chorus or the line that hits.
  • Match the pace. A slow sunset video needs a calm track; a group photo after a party needs something upbeat.
  • Let the words fit the picture. If the song has lyrics, pick a line that says what your photo is about.
  • Keep text short. With music playing, three or four words on screen are enough.

Song ideas by mood

  • Morning chai or a calm walk: something soft and acoustic.
  • Friends, trips and parties: something upbeat you can all sing along to.
  • Missing someone or a rainy evening: a slow song with a line that says it for you.
  • Festivals: something with dhol, bells or a festive beat.
  • Workouts and wins: anything with a strong beat.

Need ideas for what to post with the song? Try our 70 status ideas for every mood.

Why music makes Stories better

People watch a Story for a couple of seconds. Music makes those seconds feel like a moment instead of just a picture. It also tells your friends how you feel without you having to write it. That's why a short, honest photo with the right song often gets more replies than a perfect photo with none.
