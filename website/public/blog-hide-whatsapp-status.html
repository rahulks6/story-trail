How to Hide WhatsApp Status From Someone (or Everyone)

24 November 2026

Want to post a status without your boss, a relative or an ex seeing it? WhatsApp lets you hide your status from one person, a few people, or show it only to a chosen list. Here's exactly how, on Android and iPhone, plus what "Only share with" means and what the other person sees.

Status privacy: the three options

WhatsApp gives you three choices for who sees your status:

  • My contacts: everyone saved in your phone who also has your number saved.
  • My contacts except…: everyone except the people you pick. Use this to hide your status from one person or a few people.
  • Only share with…: only the people you pick. Use this to show your status to a small, trusted group.

How to hide WhatsApp status from one person

On Android

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to the Updates tab.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right and choose Status privacy.
  3. Choose My contacts except….
  4. Search for the person and tick their name. You can pick more than one.
  5. Tap the tick to save.

On iPhone

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to Settings > Privacy > Status. You can also open it from the Updates tab.
  2. Choose My contacts except….
  3. Select the person, then tap Done.

From now on, the people you picked won't see your new status updates. Menu names can differ slightly between WhatsApp versions.

How to share your status with only a few people

Follow the same steps, but choose Only share with… and pick the people who should see it. Everyone else won't see your status at all.

What does "Only share with" mean?

It means your status is shown only to the contacts you select, like a close friends list. Anyone you didn't select can't see it, even if they're in your contacts.

How to hide your status from everyone

There's no single "hide from everyone" switch, but you have two simple ways:

  • Choose Only share with… and select nobody, or just yourself if your number is saved. Your status will be seen by no one.
  • Or simply don't post, and delete any status that's already up (see below).

Important: privacy only applies to new updates

Changing your status privacy affects updates you post after the change. A status that's already up keeps the audience it had when you posted it.

To remove a status that's already posted:

  1. Go to the Updates tab.
  2. Tap the three dots next to My status (or tap My status).
  3. Choose the update and tap Delete.

Will the person know I hid my status?

No. WhatsApp doesn't send any notification. They simply won't see your new status updates. They might notice if they used to see your status often and suddenly see nothing.

How to hide that you've seen someone's status

If you don't want people to know you viewed their status, turn off read receipts: Settings > Privacy > Read receipts. Keep in mind it works both ways: you also won't see who viewed your status. It doesn't change read receipts in group chats.

How to stop seeing someone's status

You don't need to hide anything; just mute them. In the Updates tab, press and hold their status and tap Mute. Their updates move to the muted section, and they're not told.

Quick answers

  • Hide from one person: Status privacy > My contacts except…
  • Show only to a few people: Status privacy > Only share with…
  • Hide from everyone: Only share with… and select nobody, or delete your status
  • Already posted? Delete it; the new setting only applies to future updates
  • Does the person get a notification? No
