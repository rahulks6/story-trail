4 Ways to Share Stories Only With Close Friends

23 November 2026

Not every moment is for everyone. Maybe you want your college gang to see last night's photos, but not your relatives. Maybe you just want to post more often without feeling watched. Here are four ways to share Stories and status updates with only the people you choose, and what each one is good at.

1. Instagram Close Friends

How it works: You build one Close Friends list. When you share a Story, you can send it to that list instead of all your followers. People on the list see a green ring around your Story.

How to set it up: Go to your profile, open the menu, tap Close Friends and add people. When posting a Story, tap Close Friends instead of Your Story.

Good for: people whose friends are all on Instagram already.

Keep in mind: you have one list for everything. Instagram is also built for reach, so your feed and Explore still fill up with strangers, creators and ads.

2. WhatsApp status privacy

How it works: Your status goes to your contacts. In status privacy you can choose My contacts, My contacts except…, or Only share with… a list you pick.

How to set it up: Open WhatsApp, go to Settings > Privacy > Status and choose an option. It applies to status updates you post after changing it.

Good for: reaching family and friends who already use WhatsApp every day.

Keep in mind: it's tied to your phone contacts, so the plumber and your landlord are "contacts" too unless you exclude them. One setting covers all your updates, so you have to change it each time you want a different audience.

3. Snapchat private Stories

How it works: You can create a private Story and choose exactly which friends can view it.

Good for: friend groups that already chat on Snapchat.

Keep in mind: if your friends aren't on Snapchat, there's no one to share with.

4. Katkee

How it works: Katkee is a Stories app built around small audiences. For every Story, you choose who sees it before you share: Everyone, Followers or Close Friends. Your Home shows Stories from the people you follow first; suggestions only appear once you've watched them all.

What else it has:

  • Music on every Story, from a library of songs licensed for sharing
  • Chats, voice notes, group chats and video calls with the same people
  • Highlights, to keep your favourite Stories on your profile after the 24 hours
  • A theme of the day in the evening, when you don't know what to post
  • The app in English, Hindi, Marathi and six more languages

Good for: people who want a calmer place to share their day with their own circle.

Keep in mind: Katkee is new, so your friends may not be on it yet. You can invite them with your own invite link from Settings > Invite friends. Katkee is for people 18 and older, and is free on Android.

Which one should you use?

  • If your friends are all on one app already, start there and use its privacy settings.
  • If you want to post more often without everyone watching, a separate space for close friends helps. That's the idea behind Katkee.
  • Whatever you use, keep your list small and review it now and then. The fewer people, the more you'll actually share.
