Inside the app

Six tabs. Everything you need.

Home

Stories from people you follow, with new creators near you mixed in.

  • Full-screen Stories, photo and video
  • Like, comment and share
  • A fresh mix every time you open it

Search

Find people by username or name.

  • Suggested people to follow
  • Follow without leaving your results

Create

Capture the moment, as it is, and add a song from Katkee's free music library.

  • Full-screen camera for photos and video
  • Flash, timer, front and back camera
  • Add from your gallery
  • Text, drawing and stickers

Activity

Everything that happened while you were away, in one place.

  • Likes, comments and mentions
  • New followers and follow requests
  • You choose what notifies you

Messages

Private conversations that arrive the moment they're sent.

  • Share a Story into a chat
  • Only the people in the chat can read it

Profile

Your Highlights, three to a row. Clean and simple.

  • A ring on your photo when you have a Story
  • Highlights keep Stories past 24 hours
  • Follow or message from any profile

Safety and privacy

You decide who sees your Stories.

Katkee is built so your Stories reach the people you choose, and nothing you share is sold.

Read our Privacy Policy

  • Private account

    Only followers you approve see your Stories.

  • Your viewers are yours

    Everyone sees how many people viewed a Story. Only you see who.

  • Block, mute and report

    From any profile, Story, comment or message. Reports go to our moderation team.

  • Nearby is optional

    A city-sized area, never your exact location, and only if you turn it on.
