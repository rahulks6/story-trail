Katkee Highlights: Keep Your Best Stories on Your Profile

24 November 2026

Stories on Katkee disappear after 24 hours. That's what makes them easy to post. But some moments deserve to stay: a trip with friends, your sister's wedding, a festival at home. Highlights keep them on your profile for as long as you want.

What are Highlights?

A Highlight is a collection of your Stories, with a title, shown in a row on your profile. People who visit your profile can tap a Highlight to watch its Stories, like it, comment on it and share it.

Think of them as small albums: "Goa 2026", "College days", "Diwali at home", "My cooking experiments".

How to create a Highlight

From your profile

  1. Go to your profile.
  2. In the Highlights row, tap New.
  3. Choose the Stories you want. You can pick from any Story you've ever shared, even old ones that have disappeared.
  4. Give your Highlight a title.
  5. Tap Create.

From your Archive

Open your Archive (the button at the top of your profile), open a Story and tap Add to Highlight. Choose an existing Highlight or start a new one.

How to edit a Highlight

On your profile, press and hold a Highlight to edit or delete it. You can:

  • add more Stories to it
  • remove Stories from it
  • change its title
  • reorder your Highlights

Who can see your Highlights?

Your Highlights show on your profile, so whoever can see your profile can see them. If your account is private, only your approved followers can. A Story you shared to Close Friends stays limited to Close Friends, even in a Highlight.

Your Archive

Every Story you share is kept privately in your Archive, even after it disappears from your Story. Only you can see it. That's where you find old Stories to add to a Highlight. Open it with the Archive button at the top of your profile.

Ideas for Highlights

  • Trips: one Highlight per trip, named after the place
  • Festivals: Diwali, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Eid, Christmas
  • Your people: "The gang", "Family", "Hostel life"
  • Your interests: food, sketches, cricket, music, books
  • Firsts: first job, first bike, first solo trip
  • A year: "2026", with one Story from every month

Tips

  • Pick a few, not all. Five strong Stories make a better Highlight than fifty.
  • Use short titles. One or two words read best under the circle.
  • Keep them fresh. Add new Highlights as life happens, and remove ones that no longer feel like you.
