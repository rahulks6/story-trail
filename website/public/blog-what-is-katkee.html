What Is Katkee? The Stories App for Your Own People

24 November 2026

Katkee is a free app for sharing Stories and status with the people who matter to you. You post photos and videos with music, they disappear after 24 hours, and you choose exactly who sees each one. It also has chats, voice notes and video calls, so the conversation that starts with a Story keeps going in the same place.

Why Katkee exists

Most social apps today are built for reach. The more strangers see your post, the better they work for advertisers. That's fine for creators, but it changes how ordinary people post. When your boss, your relatives and people you met once are all watching, you start performing instead of sharing.

Katkee is built the other way around. It's for the moments you'd share with your friends at a chai tapri, not with the whole internet.

What you can do on Katkee

Share Stories with music

Take a photo or video, add a song from Katkee's music library, write a few words, add stickers or emoji, and share. Songs are sorted by mood, like Happy, Chill, Fun, Energetic and Romantic, and you pick the exact part that plays.

Choose who sees every Story

Before you share, you choose the audience:

  • Everyone: anyone on Katkee
  • Followers: only people who follow you
  • Close Friends: only the people on your Close Friends list

You decide this for each Story, so one post can go to everyone and the next only to your gang. Here's how to make your Close Friends list.

Keep your best moments as Highlights

Stories disappear after 24 hours, but you can save your favourites in Highlights on your profile: a trip, a festival, a birthday. Learn more in Katkee Highlights.

Chat, send voice notes and video call

Reply to a Story and you're in a chat. Send messages, voice notes, photos, make group chats, or start a voice or video call with the same people you share Stories with.

Fun stickers

Add polls, quizzes, questions, countdowns, GIFs and mentions to a Story, or start an Add Yours chain that friends join with their own Stories.

A theme of the day

Don't know what to post? Every evening Katkee suggests one simple theme for the day, like your chai moment or today's sky, so there's always an easy idea.

In your language

Katkee works in English, Hindi, Marathi, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French, German and Arabic. You can change the language anytime in Settings > Language.

Safety and privacy

  • Katkee is for people 18 and older.
  • You can make your account private, so people must ask to follow you.
  • You can block, mute or report anyone, from their profile or their Story.
  • Every Story and profile photo is checked automatically for nudity and sexual content, and accounts that post it are suspended.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

Read more on our Help Center and in the Privacy Policy.

Who is Katkee for?

Katkee is for anyone who wants a calmer place to share their day: college friends, cousins spread across cities, an office group that actually likes each other, a family that wants more than forwards. If you've ever thought "I'd post this, but not for everyone", Katkee is for you.

How to get started

  1. Download Katkee from Google Play. It's free.
  2. Sign up with Google in a few seconds.
  3. Add a profile photo and follow a few people.
  4. Post your first Story, with a song.
  5. Invite your closest friends with your invite link.

Our beginner's guide to Katkee walks you through every step.

Katkee is made in India by a small, independent team. We read every piece of feedback: you can send it from Settings > Send feedback in the app.
