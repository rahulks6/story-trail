Invite contest

Invite your friends, win a ₹1,000 Amazon voucher

Whoever brings the most friends to Katkee with their invite link between 23 November and 21 December 2026 wins a ₹1,000 Amazon.in gift voucher.

How to take part

  1. In the Katkee app, tap the 🏆 on Home, or go to Settings > Invite friends, and share your invite link.
  2. Your friend opens your link, installs Katkee from it and signs up.
  3. Your friend counts for you once they have used Katkee on 3 different days. The app shows each friend's progress and your place on the leaderboard.

Rules

  • Dates: friends must join from your link between 23 November 2026 and 21 December 2026 (India time). Their days on Katkee count until 24 December 2026. The winner is announced in the app by 26 December 2026.
  • Who can take part: anyone with a Katkee account who lives in India. Katkee team members and their accounts can't take part. Taking part is free; nothing has to be bought.
  • Who counts as a friend: a real person who creates a new Katkee account from your invite link and uses it on 3 different days. Accounts that are deleted, suspended or restricted for breaking the Terms of Service don't count.
  • Winning: the person with the most counted friends wins. If two people have the same number, the one who reached it first wins.
  • Prize: one ₹1,000 Amazon.in gift voucher, sent by email or in-app message to the winner within 7 days of the announcement. It can't be exchanged for cash.
  • Fair play: making fake accounts, using other people's phones or emails, paying people to sign up, or any other trick to raise your count disqualifies you. We check the winner's friends before sending the prize, and we may ask the winner to confirm their identity.
  • Not sponsored: the contest is run by Katkee alone. It isn't sponsored, endorsed or run by Amazon, Google, Meta, Facebook or Instagram.
  • Changes: we may change or end the contest if it is being abused or for reasons beyond our control; we will say so in the app and on this page.

What others see

The leaderboard in the app shows the top 10 inviters' names, usernames and profile photos, and how many friends counted for them. Your friends' names are shown only to you. Questions? Write to {{contact.support}}.
