cropped to 1080 x 1920.

The Pexels licence (pexels.com/license) allows free commercial use without attribution. It does
not allow showing identifiable people or brands as if they endorse Katkee, so photos with
recognisable faces or legible brand names are not used. The other photos in the screenshots
are downloaded during the run from Unsplash (unsplash.com/license, also free for commercial use),
by the photo ids in `.github/scripts/android-walkthrough.sh`.
